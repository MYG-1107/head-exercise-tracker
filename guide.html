Quick guide

How to use the head movement tracker

A few steps to track a movement cycle and understand the live count.

How to use the tracker

  1. Choose a mode. Camera mode estimates direction using facial landmarks. Manual mode does not request camera access.
  2. Start and calibrate. In camera mode, allow access only after pressing Start. Look comfortably toward the camera and hold a neutral position while the tracker calibrates.
  3. Complete one cycle. Move gently in one direction, then return to centre. The counter updates only after the return is detected.
  4. Review or export. See the totals by direction. Use Export summary to download a CSV to your device.

If the count does not change

  • Check that the camera permission is granted and your face is clearly in frame.
  • Use even lighting and keep the camera steady at eye level.
  • Recalibrate while facing forward, then try one slow movement and return to centre.
  • If tracking still feels unreliable, use manual mode. Camera tracking is an estimate and may vary across devices.

What it is for

This head movement counter helps people record completed movement cycles. It does not recommend which movements to perform, assess range of motion, or confirm exercise technique. It is not a medical or physiotherapy tool.
