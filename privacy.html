Privacy

Your data stays with you

No account is required. Camera tracking runs in your browser; session counts are not stored by the app.

Camera frames

The camera is requested only after you start camera mode. This app passes video frames to the tracking library inside your browser and includes no feature that intentionally uploads photos or frames to a server.

External services

Camera mode loads the MediaPipe tracking library and model assets from jsDelivr. The Source Sans 3 font is requested from NAV's public CDN. Those providers may receive standard connection metadata, such as your IP address, requested asset URL and browser information. This site cannot control their independent service logs.

Session counts and export

Counts and elapsed time remain in page memory. They are not saved to an account, cookie, local-storage record, or app database. They clear when the page is refreshed or closed. If you press Export summary, your browser downloads a CSV file to your device; the app does not upload that file.

Offline support

After the page has loaded online, a service worker can cache the app's static files so that the interface and manual mode remain available offline. This browser cache stores website files, not camera frames or session counts. Camera tracking still needs an internet connection to load its external model assets.

Your choice

You can choose manual mode at any time when not in a running camera session. It does not request camera permission or load MediaPipe. For stricter privacy requirements, avoid camera mode or review and self-host every external dependency.
