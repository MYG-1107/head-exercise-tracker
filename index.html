Your data, your device

Privacy is the default.

There is no login, analytics tool, database, cookie-based tracking, or image-upload feature in this version. Camera frames are passed to the in-browser tracker only. If you select camera mode, third-party tracking code and model assets are downloaded from jsDelivr; those requests may reveal standard connection metadata to the CDN. Manual mode does not request camera permission or load those libraries.

Please take care

Move gently and stay comfortable.

This tracker is a general-purpose activity aid, not a medical device or physiotherapy plan.

✓ Use a comfortable range of movement and take breaks whenever needed.

✓ Stop if movement causes pain, dizziness, numbness or other concerning symptoms.

✓ Seek guidance from a qualified healthcare professional if you have an injury, symptoms or questions about suitable movements.

The tracker estimates direction from facial landmarks. It does not measure clinical range of motion, posture quality, pain, recovery, or exercise correctness. Tracking accuracy has not been clinically validated.

Privacy details

What this version does with data

Camera and image data

The camera is requested only after you press Start in camera mode. This app's code passes video frames to the MediaPipe tracker running in your browser. The app includes no function that uploads or stores frames or photographs. You can stop the session to release the camera.

External dependency

When camera mode starts, version-pinned MediaPipe scripts and related model files are retrieved from jsDelivr. The CDN may receive normal connection information such as your IP address, request URL and user-agent. This version does not send video frames to jsDelivr as an upload. For stricter supply-chain isolation, the libraries and all model assets should be self-hosted and reviewed.

Session statistics

Counts, selected goal and elapsed time are kept in page memory only. They are not written to local storage, cookies, an account or a server. Refreshing or closing the page clears them.

Voice prompts

Voice prompts are off by default. If enabled, the browser's speech synthesis speaks short prompts; this app does not record microphone audio. Voice availability depends on your browser and device.

Limitations

This is a prototype. No independent security audit or clinical validation is claimed. Do not use it as a diagnosis, treatment recommendation or substitute for professional advice.

Safety information

Know the limits

This app counts detected movement cycles or manual entries. It does not diagnose a condition, determine whether an exercise is appropriate for you, measure medical range of motion or confirm correct technique. Choose a modest personal goal, move only within a comfortable range, and stop if you experience pain, dizziness or concerning symptoms. Ask a qualified healthcare professional for advice about your own situation.
