Privacy

Camera starts only when you choose it. Frames are processed in this browser; this app has no image-upload code or session-history storage. Camera mode downloads MediaPipe code and model assets from jsDelivr. The font is loaded from NAV's public CDN. Those providers may receive standard connection metadata such as your IP address and browser details. Manual mode does not use the camera or load MediaPipe.

Safety

This is a movement tracker, not a medical device or treatment plan. Move only within a comfortable range. Stop if you feel pain, dizziness, numbness or other concerning symptoms. Ask a qualified healthcare professional about movements appropriate for your situation.
