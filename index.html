Privacy at a glance

Camera frames are processed in this browser; the app has no image-upload feature. Counts stay in page memory unless you explicitly download a summary. The app shell is cached on this device to support offline manual mode. Camera tracking assets are loaded from jsDelivr and the font from NAV's public CDN; those providers may receive standard connection metadata. Full privacy details.

Safety at a glance

This is a movement tracker, not a medical device or treatment plan. Move comfortably and stop if you feel pain, dizziness, numbness or other concerning symptoms. Safety and limitations.
