How It Works

  1. Enable Camera: Click "Turn Camera On".
  2. Follow Direction Prompts: Move your head UP, DOWN, LEFT, or RIGHT.
  3. Track Expression & Reps: Maintain facial expressions and complete repetition cycles.
  4. Summary Hold: Hold center focus for 5 seconds to get an audio repetition summary.
