How It Works

  1. Enable Camera: Click "Turn Camera On" and grant browser permissions.
  2. Follow Direction Prompts: Move your head UP, DOWN, LEFT, or RIGHT as guided by the audio prompts.
  3. Track Repetitions: Complete full movement cycles to register repetitions.
  4. Hold Center for Summary: Face forward at the center for 5 seconds to hear your completed repetition count.
