About Head Exercise Tracker

Head Exercise Tracker is an interactive, browser-native AI application engineered to assist users with physical therapy, neck mobility drills, and ergonomic posture corrections.

Core Capabilities

  • Real-time 3D Facial Tracking: Utilizes MediaPipe's 468-point 3D landmark mesh to calculate head yaw, pitch, and tilt angles without external hardware.
  • Facial Expression Recognition: Evaluates geometric facial feature ratios in real time to detect engagement, focus, smiling, and breathing states.
  • Voice-Guided Repetition Counting: Hands-free audio cues announce directional shifts and summarize complete workout sets.

Designed under clean Scandinavian UI principles prioritizing clarity, contrast, and accessibility.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Your privacy is paramount. The Head Exercise Tracker application is built with client-side edge computing principles:

  • Local Frame Processing: Video feeds from your webcam are processed locally in real time inside your browser memory using MediaPipe WebAssembly modules.
  • No Continuous Video Storage: Raw webcam video streams are never recorded, streamed to central application servers, or sold to third parties.
  • Analytics: Standard anonymized website usage statistics are captured via Google Analytics to monitor application performance and stability.

Health & Safety Disclaimer

Important Notice: The Head Exercise Tracker is an automated educational tool designed to assist with movement counting and general mobility awareness.

This software does not constitute professional medical advice, diagnosis, or physical therapy treatment. Consult a physician or certified physical therapist before beginning any new exercise routine, especially if you have pre-existing cervical spine conditions, neck injury, or chronic pain.
