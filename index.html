Privacy details

The camera is requested only after you press Start in camera mode. This application does not contain image-upload, analytics, cookie, or session-persistence features. Counts and elapsed time live only in page memory and clear when you refresh or close the page.

Camera mode loads version-pinned MediaPipe tracking code and model assets from jsDelivr. The CDN may receive standard connection metadata such as IP address, requested asset URLs and user-agent. Frames are passed to the tracker in this browser, not intentionally uploaded. Third-party code is part of the camera-processing path; for a stricter supply-chain boundary, self-host and review the library and model assets before release.

Manual mode does not request camera permission or load MediaPipe. Voice prompts are off by default and use browser speech synthesis; microphone audio is not recorded.

Safety and limitations

This app estimates movement direction from facial landmarks or records movements entered manually. It does not diagnose a condition, measure clinical range of motion, verify technique, or prescribe exercise.

Choose a modest goal, move gently and take breaks. Stop if movement causes pain, dizziness, numbness or other concerning symptoms. Ask a qualified healthcare professional about movements appropriate for your situation.

Direction detection varies with camera placement, lighting, device performance and individual movement. If counts do not appear reliable, stop camera tracking and use manual mode.
