How It Works

  1. Enable Camera: Click "Turn Camera On" and grant browser permissions.
  2. Follow Direction Prompts: Move your head UP, DOWN, LEFT, or RIGHT as guided.
  3. Track Repetitions: Complete full movement cycles to register repetitions.
  4. Hold Center for Summary: Hold center focus for 5 seconds to hear your repetition summary.

About Head Exercise Tracker

Head Exercise Tracker is a web-based artificial intelligence tool designed to assist with neck mobility, physical therapy, and ergonomic posture breaks. Using real-time computer vision landmark detection directly inside your browser, no video data is sent to any server.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is completely protected. All camera video processing occurs strictly on your local device within the client web browser. No video, audio, or spatial tracking data is uploaded, recorded, or transmitted to any remote servers.

Medical Disclaimer

This web application is intended for fitness tracking and wellness support only. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult with a qualified healthcare provider before beginning any physical therapy or neck exercise routine.
