Privacy details

Camera access is requested only after you press Start in camera mode. This app passes frames to the MediaPipe library in your browser and contains no image-upload or session-persistence feature. Counts and timer values exist only in page memory and clear when you refresh or close the page.

Camera mode downloads version-pinned scripts and model assets from jsDelivr. The CDN may receive normal connection metadata such as IP address, requested asset URLs and user-agent. The app does not intentionally send video frames to the CDN. Manual mode does not request camera access or load MediaPipe.

Voice prompts are off by default and use browser speech synthesis only; the app does not record microphone audio.

Safety and limitations

This app estimates direction from facial landmarks or records movements you enter manually. It does not measure clinical range of motion, verify correct technique, diagnose an illness or prescribe exercise.

Choose a modest goal, move gently and take breaks. Stop if movement causes pain, dizziness, numbness or other concerning symptoms. Ask a qualified healthcare professional about movements appropriate for your situation.
