Safety

Move comfortably. Know the limits.

This is a movement counter, not a medical device or physiotherapy plan.

Use only comfortable movement

This site counts movements; it does not tell you which exercises are appropriate for you. Do not force a movement or try to reach a target through discomfort.

Stop when you need to

Stop if you feel pain, dizziness, numbness, weakness or other concerning symptoms. Ask a qualified healthcare professional for advice if you have an injury, ongoing symptoms, or questions about appropriate movement.

Know what the tracker can measure

Camera mode estimates relative direction from facial landmarks. It can miss or misclassify movement due to lighting, camera angle, movement speed, device performance or face-tracking interruptions. Manual mode records what the user enters; it does not verify movement.

The app does not diagnose a condition, measure clinical range of motion, assess posture quality, recommend treatment, or confirm correct technique. It is not a medical device and has not been clinically validated.
